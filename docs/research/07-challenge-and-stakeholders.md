### 1.3 What should shape the demo and video (INFERRED from the above)

1. Show agency, not a chatbot: the system perceives events, decides (gate, estimate), acts (SMS, call, nurse alert), and logs. Name the agents/tools on screen.
2. Show "for Good" with a measurable societal outcome: 10.02% left without being seen in NS in 2023-24 (cited in the design doc; corroborated in section 3.1).
3. Show responsible AI visibly: consent, fail-closed gate, impostor refusal, audit log. This matches the stated challenge goal of "responsible technology use".
4. Be explicit that data is synthetic and the OPOR feed is simulated in Oracle formats; do not imply NS Health endorsement.
5. Close with a consulting-style path to adoption (section 2) and cost per visit (section 3). Cognizant is a services firm; a deployment story matters.

### 3.5 Strongest ROI framing

1. **Walkouts.** About 52,400 NS patients left unseen in 2023-24 (calculation above); about 7,000+ at the Infirmary. Even a 5% relative reduction is thousands of patients who stay for care. Dollar value per LWBS not found; do not invent one. LEAVING-to-nurse-alert gives a countable pilot outcome. Evidence base is vendor-reported (Vital) plus small studies; an urgent-care poster found automated text outreach reached many more patients than manual calls (4,394 texted, 76.8% engaged, versus 158 of 557 reached by phone). https://urgentcareassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/POSTER-Mangas-HuntDont-Lose-the-Patient-Amplifying-Access.pdf . CONFIRMED (summary), design differs from ours.
2. **Nurse and clerk interruptions.** Waiting-room "where am I / how long" questions are plausibly large but I found no published NS or Canadian measurement. UNCERTAIN; pilot should measure baseline call/walk-up counts (already in the design doc).
3. **Complaints and Patient Relations load.** Plausible, unmeasured. UNCERTAIN.
4. **Ties to stated provincial goals.** Action for Health already frames C3 real-time information, virtual ED care and YourHealthNS as ways to cut ED demand and wait impact; VeriCare fits as the patient-facing layer. https://actionforhealth.novascotia.ca/emergency-care-plan . INFERRED fit.

## Design implications for VeriCare

1. **Pitch the pathway, not a deal.** Intake form at the Innovation Hub, with an NS Health ED/C3 champion as pilot lead; mention the Hub IP statement (IP stays with the rights holder) and the DHW data-sharing condition so judges see we read the terms. Do not claim an API into OPOR or YourHealthNS; say integration is via NS Health's own feed (the design doc already does).
2. **Evidence caution is real.** Dalhousie's review found no evidence that publishing wait estimates reduces crowding. Frame the pilot as measuring LWBS, calls and range coverage, not as a crowding fix. Keep the escalation line in every message.
3. **Cost slide:** "about C$0.50 per enrolled visit in usage fees (range C$0.30-0.80); about C$35k/year if every Halifax Infirmary visit were enrolled" with assumptions on screen. Label the Infirmary volume as an estimate.
4. **Name roles, not people** for NS Health leadership (currency uncertain). Say "Central Zone ED program, C3, Patient Family Advisors" in the roadmap.
5. **Demo for Cognizant judges:** visible multi-step agent loop (event, decision, action, log), responsible-AI gate and impostor refusal, simulator clearly labelled synthetic, then a 10-second "path to pilot" and cost card. Keep the 52,400 / 10.02% hook in the first 20 seconds.
6. **Cognizant angle:** FHIR is common ground with their TriZetto direction; say the same event model could sit behind an agent gateway, but do not claim TriZetto relevance to ED flow.
7. **Resilience to vendor change:** OpenAI will retire older realtime models on 2027-01-20 (per cited article); keep the voice model name in config.
